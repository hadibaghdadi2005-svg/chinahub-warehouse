How to use

1. Scan a shelf barcode. It appears at the top as the current shelf.

2. Scan every parcel on that shelf. Each one is recorded against it.

3. Scan the next shelf barcode and carry on.

Photos (for Sale stock, only in No shelf mode): press No shelf, then Photo so it is lit. After each parcel scan the camera opens; take the picture. You can also tap the camera box next to any parcel to take or retake its photo. Photos stay on this device. To send them, press Export → Save with photos and import that file in the warehouse app under Sale stock → Import from PDA.

Parcels only, no shelf (for example for Sale stock): press No shelf, then scan the parcels. They are recorded with no shelf. Scanning any shelf barcode, or pressing the button again, goes back to shelves.

Short codes are treated as shelves or zones (ZONE-SHIP, C-01-02…), long mostly-numeric codes as parcels. Shipping-client labels (SHIP-123) count as goods: scan a zone or shelf first, then the SHIP labels in it. If a code is scanned twice it is counted once and flagged as a repeat.

Everything is stored on this device, so you can close the page and come back. Nothing is sent anywhere.

When finished, press Export, then Copy all, and paste it into the warehouse app under Setup → Import. Set "These rows are" to Already on shelves.
