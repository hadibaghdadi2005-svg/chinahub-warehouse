How to use

1. Scan a shelf barcode. It appears at the top as the current shelf.

2. Scan every parcel on that shelf. Each one is recorded against it.

3. Scan the next shelf barcode and carry on.

Short codes are treated as shelves, long mostly-numeric codes as parcels. If a parcel is scanned twice it is counted once and flagged as a repeat.

Everything is stored on this device, so you can close the page and come back. Nothing is sent anywhere.

When finished, press Export, then Copy all, and paste it into the warehouse app under Setup → Import. Set "These rows are" to Already on shelves.
